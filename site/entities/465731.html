Notes

From REED vol. 1, xii | INTRODUCTION: "Some revenue came under the jurisdiction of four bridgemasters, whose year of office also began 3 February until 1491 when they took office on 15 January. They collected rents from properties on and adjacent to Ouse Bridge and Foss Bridge. Any funds remaining after the necessary bridge maintenance had been attended to were handed over to the chamberlains. The office of bridgemaster was abolished in 1626."
