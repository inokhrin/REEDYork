Notes

In the first year of Henry VII's reign the king and the earl of Northumberland put forward candidates for the post of recorder of York, but the corporation defended its autonomy and elected Vavasour (who had acted for the city for some years) in spring 1486. He continued in office until his appointment as puisne justice of the common pleas on 17 October 1489. He was knighted in 1501. https://doi.org/10.1093/ref:odnb/28167
