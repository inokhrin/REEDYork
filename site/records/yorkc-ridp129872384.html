Yorkshire West Riding, York — 1491
Source description
YCDHBHouse Books
Settlement: York
Repository: NYCRO
Shelfmark: B 5: 1483-9

Technical description: English, some Latin and Anglo-Norman in early volumes; paper (except B9, parchment); number of leaves varies from 41 (B12) to 402 (B32); smallest B30 (290mm x 180mm), largest B11 (395mm x 275/280mm), text in a single, wide, right-hand column, amount of leaf covered varies from scribe to scribe; gathered irregularly (the majority in 8s); modern foliation; little ornamentation in script until B29, ruled margins B29-B36, most marginal headings contemporary with the text; bound in white paper-covered boards resembling leather, shelf-mark gilt on red, spines of B30-36 more ornamental. All books damaged and repaired (damage most severe in early books, B1-B7 rectos badly faded, B2-4 missing first 3 leaves, last 2 gatherings B9 reversed, B20 missing ff 5-6, B28 opening leaves foliated A-U, some interleaving of letters and other single documets).

f 38 (19 February)

[folio 38]

[…]

To oure trusty and welbeloued Counseillour and Knyght for oure body sir Richard Tunstall and to the Maire of oure Citie of York.

By the kyng

Trusty and welbeloued we grete you wele And for Asmoche as we bee enformed that grete diuisions discordes and debates depend & of long tyme passed haue doon betwix thartificers of wevers and Cordeners reseant and dwelling within oure Citie ther for their going with torches in procession wherby often tymes thynges haue been like to be atto attempted to þe peruersion & breche of oure peas and inquietacion of oure subgiettes of oure seid Citie We therfor entending good and peasiable rule to be obserued & kept ther without any thing to be meoved to the contrarie wol and desire you that calling before you thartificers of both crafftez ye will haue this matier in good and deliberat examycion examynacion and therupon ordre & and sed surely establisshe suche a direccion therein according to the olde vsages accostomed there as may be for þe fynall appeasing of the said debates without any furthere trouble or demaunde to be made in that behalf And þat this passe you not vndirected as we truste you and as we haue no cause tare tarrecte or ascribe any default vnto you hereafter Yeuen vnder oure signet at oure

palois of westminster the xixth day of feurier

At whice day it Assembled in þe counseill chambre opon Ousebryge ther was Rede þe kynges lettre myssyve dyrected to master sir Richard Tunstall & Mylord Maier for reformacion of þe variaunce betwix þe weuers & Cordweners of þis Citie for beryng of Torchez on þe morn after corpus christi day þe tenour wheroff ensuyth hereafter […]

f 40 (14 May)

[folio 1] […]

Item the same day the Serssours of þe Cordweners come afore þe said presence & dissired to haue ‸ þe ordinauncez ‸ of their craft agayn deliuered and to bere theyr torchez in theyr old rome & place þe morne after corpus christi day and accordyng to the kynges k lettrez whiche was opynly rede afore þe seid presence wherunto þe seid maire & presence ‸ answerd & comaunded theym to bere theyr torchez ather by theym self on both sidez the Strete afore the wevers or elles to go on be lefft hand & þe wevers on þe right hand or eles to go make fyne with þe Chambre and be dysmysed of beryng any torches ‸ for this yere and to giff yerly ‸ hereafter to þe Chamberleyns for tyme beyng xiij s iiij d for to haue theyr ordinaunces agayn with serche of rede lether & blake […] heropon the seid serssourz in þe nam[…] of þe hole […] crafft askes respyte of theyr answer in that behalue to þe next assemble of the seid presence as after ensuyth

f 40v(30 May)

[folio 1] […]

at which day þe Serssours of þe Cordweners gaf answer þat theyr craft was agreed to pay & content yerly vnto þe chamberleyns for tyme beyng xiij s. iiij d to haue þer old ordynauncez agayn deliuered with serche of blake & rede lether and all other correcionz & punyschmentes & forfaitez longyng to þer seid craft without interupcion of any other in apayre Indenturz vnder seale of office of Mayraltie & þe sessours of the same crafte enterchaungeably whiche was grant theym by all þe seid presence and they to bere theyre torchez þe morn after corpus christi day in oone of þe two rommez afore reherced apon þe penaltie afore þis þis tyme þerrin ordaned
