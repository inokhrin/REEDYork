Source description
YCDHBHouse Books
Settlement: York
Repository: NYCRO
Shelfmark: B 5: 1483-9

Technical description: English, some Latin and Anglo-Norman in early volumes; paper (except B9, parchment); number of leaves varies from 41 (B12) to 402 (B32); smallest B30 (290mm x 180mm), largest B11 (395mm x 275/280mm), text in a single, wide, right-hand column, amount of leaf covered varies from scribe to scribe; gathered irregularly (the majority in 8s); modern foliation; little ornamentation in script until B29, ruled margins B29-B36, most marginal headings contemporary with the text; bound in white paper-covered boards resembling leather, shelf-mark gilt on red, spines of B30-36 more ornamental. All books damaged and repaired (damage most severe in early books, B1-B7 rectos badly faded, B2-4 missing first 3 leaves, last 2 gatherings B9 reversed, B20 missing ff 5-6, B28 opening leaves foliated A-U, some interleaving of letters and other single documets).

f 21v 15 January

[folio 21] […]

Assembled in þe Counseill Chambre within þe Comon Hall My lord Maier shewed howe þat one Roger Smalwod Mynstrall whiche is come to occupye as one of þe Mynstralles of þe Citie & is knawen right connyng & of his awn cost for þe wurship of þis Citie hase maid hym a coler to his gret charge & ouer þat he awe xiij s. iiij d of his fraunchest money & is of smale hauour & power to pay & bere þe said charge wherfor he besecheth my said lord Maier & þe said presence to be good & tender lord & mayster & to help & socour hym toward þe said charge after whiche shewyng it was agreed þat þe said Roger toward his said charge shuld be pardoned of the said xiij s. iiij d

f 25 11 April

[folio 25] […]

Item the same day lohn langton drapour come to fore þe said presence & shewed þat they war bot iijth persons & þe pagent of thayr crafft is so costly opon corpus christi day & wher þat in þe tyme of William Holbeke it was ennacted þat euery person oþer Taillour or hosier that Cutteth & Selleth cloth by retaille as a drapour doth shalbe yerely contributory to þe bryngyng furth of þe Drapour pagent accordyng vnto þe said ordnance maid in the tyme of William Holbeke

f 26 19 May

[folio 26] […]

Assembled vpon Ouse brigge my lord Maier reherced howe at the last assemble It was comoned þat all thei þat cuttes & Selles cloth by retayll shuld be contributory vnto the bryngyngfurth of þe Drapours and howe þat the Taillours asked respyte of theyr answer and thervpon William lewte Maister of þe Taillours was send for & come to fore þe said presence and said he couth giff non answere without advice of his company & oþer Counsell & þervpon he & Thomas Bankhouse departed furth of þe Counseill & after þat matere was put in respyte vnto monday next
