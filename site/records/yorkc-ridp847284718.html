Source description
YCDHBHouse Books
Settlement: York
Repository: NYCRO
Shelfmark: B 5: 1483-9

Technical description: English, some Latin and Anglo-Norman in early volumes; paper (except B9, parchment); number of leaves varies from 41 (B12) to 402 (B32); smallest B30 (290mm x 180mm), largest B11 (395mm x 275/280mm), text in a single, wide, right-hand column, amount of leaf covered varies from scribe to scribe; gathered irregularly (the majority in 8s); modern foliation; little ornamentation in script until B29, ruled margins B29-B36, most marginal headings contemporary with the text; bound in white paper-covered boards resembling leather, shelf-mark gilt on red, spines of B30-36 more ornamental. All books damaged and repaired (damage most severe in early books, B1-B7 rectos badly faded, B2-4 missing first 3 leaves, last 2 gatherings B9 reversed, B20 missing ff 5-6, B28 opening leaves foliated A-U, some interleaving of letters and other single documets).

f 101-1v

[folio 101] […]

Memorandum that wher in the tyme of the Marialtie of the Rightwurshipfull sir Richard york then Maier of the Citie of york Emong other thynges it was ennacted and Established that euery Maister Cartwright within the Citie & suburbs of the same that haith & taketh Apprentice shuld yerely pay for euery apprentice to the bryngyngfurth off the pagiaunt of Carpenters iiij d as in the said Acte more at large doith appere ffor payment wherof many & gret enconuenientes & troublez haith ben likely to afallen & ensewed betwix the said carpenters on the on partie & the Cartwrightes on the other partie of in & opon all which variauncez & troublez the said partiez the d day of the yere of the reigne of king henre the vijth after the Conquest xijth beyng personally present in the Counceill Chambre opon Ousebrige tofore the ryght wurshipfull sir William Neleson then maier of the Citie of york comprimyt theym & ther successours in & opon the premysez in all thyng to stand & fulfyll & obserue the ordinance award and Arbitriment of the said Maier the which by ryppe communicacion beinge had betwix the said partiez for consumacion of the peace luffe & Amyte betwix the said parties And in eschewyng of Inconuenientes that theron myght ensew by god deliberacion Assent & Consent of the said parties ordayned awarded & demed in maner & forme ensewyng [folio 101v]That is to say the Maister Cartwrightes of the said Citie that now ar & herafter shalbe and ther successours & euery of theym yerely before the fest of corpus Christi shall pay & content vnto the pagiaunt Maisters of the Carpenters for the yere beyng vj d & euery apprentece of þe Cartwryghtes to befree for any thyng payng toward the bryngyngfurth of the said pagiaunt Also the said Maier ordayned & demed þat the said Carpenters nor yer successours shall not vex trouble ne distreyn the said Cartwryghtes nor theyr successours nor apprenticez for Any other yerely charge of payment toward the bryngyngfurth of ther pagiaunt bot in maner & forme abouesaid & euery lournaman iiij d And the Cartwryghtes to make iiij new wheles to the said pagiaunt & yerely vj d as is toforesaid & then the said Cartwryghtes & yer successours for euer to be discharged of all maner other yerely charges as torchez reparacionz of þer pagiaunt bryngyngfurth yerof or any oyer maner yerely Charge longyng to the pagiaunt or torchez

Also the said Maier ordayned awardeth & demeth & straitly chargeth þat whether of the said partiez þat herafter breketh this ordinance award afor in any poynt or article afore rehersed to forfett & leese to the Maier & Commonialtie of this Citie xl s to be leved & takyn by the Maier & Chamberleyns for the tyme beyng & imploed toward the Common well of this Citie

f 102v 16 December

[folio 102v] […]

Assembled opon ouse brigge George Essex & Thomase Bankhouse then Shireffes of þis Citie wer sent for & come to fore þe said presence and then & ther it was obiect vnto þe said George That wher for þe honour & wurship of þis Citie it hathe ben vsed þat the Shireffes on þe day of theyr Rydyng ather of theym to haue one or two folowers after hym and þe said George þat day had non And also it hath ben vsed þe Elder Shireff þat day to fynd table clothez & towels & al oþer thyng necessary for þe high table and þe same day þer wanted double towels and wher þe comon officers as þe chamberleyns comon clerk & þe Mayrez sergiauntes hath ben yerely disired & bedyn to þe shireffes fest & þis yere wer not beden and also þat wer by an auncient ordinance it is ordeyned þat no shireff of þis Citie shall not go openly in þe strete with out one beryng a mayce tofore hym this opon payn of forfettour of […]li. as in þe same dothe appere and þis day It was obiect vnto þe said George had gon from his Awn house on þe pavement vnto henry Bluther vynter in pettergate on saynt Andrewe gate corner not hauyng any Maice born tofore hym contrary to þe provision afore said apon al whiche obieccions he couth not excuse hym and after þat It was determyned by þe said presence þat the said George for his said mysbyhauours shall lay doun tofore my lord Maier & chamberleyns v li. & þerof to be pardoned xx s. and more if my lord Maier please

At the top of f 101v is the following running head: 'xvo die Decembre anno Henrici vij'.
